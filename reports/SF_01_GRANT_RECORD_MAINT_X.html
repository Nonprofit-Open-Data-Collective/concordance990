Reviewer checklist

  • The definition matches the form line and instructions for every xpath’s schema years.
  • Every xpath pooled here measures the same thing on the 990 and 990-EZ (close enough for analysis).
  • The coverage chart has no unexplained gaps or jumps between tax years.
  • Each open flag is fixed, or accepted with a note in validation_log.csv.
  • The box means the same thing (and the same polarity) under every xpath: ‘required’ is never pooled with ‘not required’.
  • Absent means unchecked for this line, or the question was skipped; the data dictionary says which.
  • Yes/No questions coded as two separate boxes are mapped to one variable only when that is intended.
