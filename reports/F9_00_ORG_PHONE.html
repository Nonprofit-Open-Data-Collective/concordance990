Reviewer checklist

  • The definition matches the form line and instructions for every xpath’s schema years.
  • Every xpath pooled here measures the same thing on the 990 and 990-EZ (close enough for analysis).
  • The coverage chart has no unexplained gaps or jumps between tax years.
  • Each open flag is fixed, or accepted with a note in validation_log.csv.
  • The data type is text so leading zeros and letters survive.
  • The identifier belongs to the entity the variable names (the filer, preparer, related organization…).
  • Formats with and without punctuation (e.g. ZIP+4, dashes) are normalized consistently downstream.
