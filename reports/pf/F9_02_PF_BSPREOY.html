Reviewer checklist

  • The definition matches the form line and instructions for every xpath’s schema years.
  • Every xpath pooled here measures the same thing on the 990 and 990-EZ (close enough for analysis).
  • The coverage chart has no unexplained gaps or jumps between tax years.
  • Each open flag is fixed, or accepted with a note in validation_log.csv.
  • Values are whole dollars, not thousands, and not a percentage.
  • Current year / prior year and beginning / end of year match the variable name (CY, PY, BOY, EOY).
  • Negative values are expected only for net amounts (gains, net income, fund balances).
  • The 990 and 990-EZ medians differ only as much as the filer populations do.

Record the review as a row in inst/extdata/src/validation_log.csv (variable_name, reviewer, date, status, note). Evidence built (unknown) from the ef2 DuckDB builds (tax years 2023).
